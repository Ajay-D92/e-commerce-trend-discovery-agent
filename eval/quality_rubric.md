# Quality rubric for the marketing brief

Score each criterion from 1 (poor) to 5 (strong). Score the page as a reader would see it.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grounded | Mentions colours, materials or rooms not in the data | Mostly grounded, one stretch | Every idea traces to a segment or hashtag in the data |
| Specific | Restates the palette or generic advice | Some concrete ideas | Each hypothesis names a shopper situation and a testable idea |
| Honest about evidence | Weak segments get confident plans | Mixed | Weak segments get cautious, low-cost tests |
| Consistent | Themes contradict their segments | One mismatch | Every theme fits all segments it lists |
| Usable | A marketer could not act on it | Needs rewriting | A marketer could run the first test tomorrow |
